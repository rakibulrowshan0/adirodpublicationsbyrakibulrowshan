01 / THE IDEA

Publishing should begin before the book exists.

We don't want to ask only, "What books should we publish?" We want to ask: "Who could become a writer?"

There are stories everywhere. A farmer's memory. A student's question. A grandmother's history. A researcher's discovery. A migrant's journey. A child's imagination. A reader's response. Adi Rod creates a pathway for these voices to enter the publishing ecosystem.

Every story begins somewhere
Every writer begins as a learner
Every book needs a reader
Every reader carries stories
Every reader can become a writer
Every generation needs new voices

10 / TECHNOLOGY

Technology should bring people closer to reading.

AI as a writing companion.

  • Research assistance
  • Language support
  • Translation assistance
  • Structure exploration
  • Reader questions
  • Book discovery
  • Accessibility

Not a replacement for the writer.

  • Human authorship remains central
  • Editorial responsibility remains human
  • Sources should be identifiable
  • AI use should be transparent
  • Author rights should be protected
  • AI licensing should be explicit
  • Readers remain participants
